TangVision Support

Help for viewing-distance checks, activity plans, reminders and local records

Camera or distance is not working

  1. Make sure the iPhone or iPad front camera is unobstructed.
  2. Place your face inside the on-screen guide and use steady lighting.
  3. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and allow TangVision.
  4. Use Distance calibration to adjust the displayed estimate for your device position.

TangVision provides an estimate for viewing habits. It is not a clinical measuring instrument.

Reminders are not playing

  • Check Alert sound, Voice reminders and Haptic feedback in Settings.
  • Check the device volume and Silent Mode.
  • Preview the selected sound in Reminder Library before starting a session.
  • Custom recordings require microphone permission and remain on this device.

Plans and background behavior

Starting an activity plan immediately starts the foreground distance check. When TangVision goes to the background, camera detection pauses because iOS and iPadOS do not allow a normal app to continuously use the camera there. Return to TangVision to resume the active session.

TangVision Plus

Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If you purchased Plus and it is not shown, open TangVision Plus and choose Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

Privacy and data deletion

Camera frames and face data are processed locally and are not uploaded. To remove local sessions and recordings, open Settings → On-device privacy → Delete records.
